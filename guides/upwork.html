Upwork: beginner's guide

Upwork is a freelance marketplace where clients can post projects and hire independent professionals. Work is not guaranteed, and getting started may require a clear service offering, relevant examples, and time spent finding suitable projects.

How to get started

  1. Visit the official website and read the current user and freelancer terms.
  2. Check whether registration and the relevant payment options are available in your country.
  3. Create an accurate profile that describes your skills and experience.
  4. Review each project's scope, budget, client details, and application requirements before submitting a proposal.
  5. Understand contract terms and platform rules before accepting work.

Fees, eligibility and payments

Platform rules, service fees, proposal or promotion costs, available payment methods, minimum withdrawal amounts, identity checks, and country eligibility may change. Review the current official terms and the options shown in your own account before committing time or money. Do not assume a payment method is available in every country.

Things to consider

  • Some proposal or visibility features may have costs. Check the current pricing before spending.
  • Read the contract's service-fee and payment-protection rules before accepting work.
  • Avoid clients who ask you to pay upfront fees to receive a job or move suspiciously quickly to off-platform payments.
  • Never pay an unofficial person to unlock earnings or withdraw funds.
  • Do not share your password, authentication codes, or payment PIN with a supposed client or support agent.
  • Keep communication and payments within the platform when its rules require it.
  • Competition is real: creating an account does not guarantee clients, orders, or income.
