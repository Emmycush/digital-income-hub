Freecash: beginner's guide

Freecash is an online rewards platform that offers eligible users tasks such as surveys, game offers, and other promotional activities. Available offers and rewards vary by country and account.

How it works

  1. Visit the official website and read its current terms.
  2. Check whether registration and the offers you want are available in your country.
  3. Review the completion conditions before starting an offer.
  4. Keep evidence of completed offers and follow the platform's support process if a reward is missing.

Eligibility and withdrawals

Important: supported withdrawal methods, minimum amounts, verification requirements, and fees can vary by country and change over time. Check the options displayed in your own account before spending time on offers.

Risks and practical advice

  • Rewards are not guaranteed income. Surveys can screen you out and offers may have conditions.
  • Never pay an unofficial person to unlock a withdrawal.
  • Do not use false identity details or attempt to bypass country restrictions.
  • Read privacy terms before sharing personal data or installing apps.
