Safety checklist

  • Find the operator's official terms, privacy policy and support contact before connecting an account or wallet.
  • Check whether rewards are real, what withdrawal minimums and fees apply, and whether users in your country qualify.
  • Never share your Telegram login code, two-step verification password, crypto seed phrase or private key.
  • Do not send cryptocurrency or pay a fee to unlock a promised prize or withdraw a displayed balance.
  • Be cautious with messages pressuring you to invite others or promising guaranteed returns.
