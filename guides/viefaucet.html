Viefaucet: what to check first

Viefaucet is associated with online faucet and reward activities. Before using any faucet or reward service, investigate how credits are earned, whether withdrawals are currently operating, and what costs or network fees apply.

Before registering

  1. Read the current site rules and privacy information.
  2. Check account requirements and whether users in your country are supported.
  3. Confirm the current withdrawal options, minimums, fees, and processing conditions.
  4. Calculate the likely value of rewards after network or conversion fees.

Safety checklist

  • Never share a wallet seed phrase or private key.
  • Do not send funds to unlock a supposed reward or withdrawal.
  • Be cautious with wallet connections, downloads, and requests for unnecessary permissions.
  • Do not treat a displayed balance as proof that a withdrawal will succeed.
