Paidwork: beginner's guide

Paidwork promotes online earning activities that may include surveys, games, watching content, and other tasks. Available activities and reward rates can differ by country and account.

How to get started

  1. Open the official website and review the terms and privacy policy.
  2. Confirm registration and task availability in your country.
  3. Read each task's requirements before starting.
  4. Track completed tasks and retain relevant confirmation details.

Withdrawals and eligibility

Do not assume that a payment method or advertised minimum is available everywhere. Check the current withdrawal screen, currency conversion, fees, identity checks, and payment-provider requirements for your own region.

Things to consider

  • Task availability and rewards are not guaranteed.
  • Some activities may require purchases, subscriptions, or reaching a milestone. Read the conditions carefully.
  • Never share your account password or payment PIN with someone claiming to be support.
  • Do not pay a third party to release your earnings.
