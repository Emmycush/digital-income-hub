Fiverr: beginner's guide

Fiverr is a marketplace where freelancers offer defined services to potential buyers. Success depends on the service, presentation, demand, pricing, delivery quality, and competition; creating a listing does not guarantee orders.

How to get started

  1. Visit the official website and review the current seller terms and policies.
  2. Confirm that seller registration and payment options are available in your country.
  3. Choose a service you can deliver reliably and describe exactly what the buyer will receive.
  4. Set clear scope, delivery time, revision terms, and pricing.
  5. Read order, cancellation, dispute, and withdrawal rules before accepting paid work.

Fees, eligibility and payments

Platform rules, service fees, proposal or promotion costs, available payment methods, minimum withdrawal amounts, identity checks, and country eligibility may change. Review the current official terms and the options shown in your own account before committing time or money. Do not assume a payment method is available in every country.

Things to consider

  • Check the current seller fees and how much you would receive after deductions.
  • Verify withdrawal methods, minimums, processing periods, and currency-conversion costs for your region.
  • Do not accept requests to buy gift cards, send money, disclose login codes, or pay unofficial account-activation fees.
  • Never pay an unofficial person to unlock earnings or withdraw funds.
  • Do not share your password, authentication codes, or payment PIN with a supposed client or support agent.
  • Keep communication and payments within the platform when its rules require it.
  • Competition is real: creating an account does not guarantee clients, orders, or income.
