About us

Helping people research online opportunities

Digital Income Hub is being developed as an educational resource for people who want to understand digital tools, survey sites, task platforms, Telegram bots, affiliate programs, and other online opportunities.

Our approach

We aim to explain how platforms work, where eligibility and payout details are available, and what limitations users should consider. Platform terms can change, so readers should check current official information before joining.

Our standards

  • Clearly distinguish official links from referral links.
  • Do not guarantee earnings.
  • Do not publish fabricated testimonials or user statistics.
  • Prioritize original explanations and practical instructions.
